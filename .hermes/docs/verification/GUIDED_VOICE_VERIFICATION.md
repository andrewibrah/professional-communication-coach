## Operational/security decisions

- Browser WebRTC is **audio-only**. Server rejects any additional media section, SCTP/application or video in both offer and answer; no provider data channel or ephemeral credential is supplied to the browser. This follows OpenAI's server-controlled example, rather than pretending a data channel is receive-only.
- FastAPI owns canonical prompts, revisions, retry budgets and trusted saved evidence. Realtime GA `/v1/realtime/calls`, sideband `?call_id=...` and `/calls/{id}/hangup` only; GPT-Live events/examples are not mixed in.
- Wording feedback comes from finalized provider ASR and strict structured Responses. No pronunciation/prosody/pacing score or unsupported audio praise. Invalid/refused structured output is not truncated/fabricated and still consumes its trusted usage reservation.
- One active session; server duration 300 seconds; heartbeat 20 seconds; pause grace 30 seconds; at most three physical connection setups; configured retry budget 0–2; one durable preparation and twelve nonrefundable evaluation reservations; aggregate token cap 6000 across every voice/ASR/text generation. Independent local deadline guard initiates hangup without first waiting on SQL or another session cleanup. These numbers are enforcement settings, **not measured price or latency**.
- SQL watchdog/termination intent survives process restart and content/Auth deletion. Exact external completion still depends on provider/network availability; a request initiation or token expiry is not confirmed termination. Lost setup responses before a known call ID remain a proof limitation. Do not promise physically exact cutoff timing during provider outage.
- Provider output-buffer drain differs from response generation completion. Browser additionally requires successful Audio.play and a 500ms media-tail grace before enabling input. The grace is heuristic; real echo/latency/playback ordering needs live approval-gated review, including initial target delivery after negotiation.
- Show/Hide is practice presentation, not secrecy against owner network inspection. Guided private text is tab-memory only. No permanent raw Guided audio is stored by this app; provider retention is not represented as zero retention.
- Suspension, AI kill switches, JWT expiry, ownership and protected persistence readiness remain fail-closed. No fake account, service-role identity impersonation, credential changes or confirmation bypass was used.
